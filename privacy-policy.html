Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026

DG Fitness Club ("we", "us", "our") operates this website and the gym located at 2nd Floor, DG Market, Opp. Bus Stand, Nandurbar – 425412, Maharashtra, India. This policy explains what personal information we collect from you, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the enquiry form on this website or enrol as a member, we may collect:

  • Your full name
  • Your phone / WhatsApp number
  • Your email address
  • Your stated fitness goal and preferred training time
  • Any message or additional details you choose to share
  • Health or fitness information you voluntarily provide during assessment or personal training

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide only to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and share membership plan details
  • Register and manage your gym membership
  • Prepare your workout and diet plans
  • Send you updates about offers, timings, events and important gym notices
  • Maintain our internal member records

3. WhatsApp Enquiries

Our enquiry form opens WhatsApp with your details pre-filled. Nothing is transmitted until you choose to send the message. Once sent, the message is delivered to our business WhatsApp number and is also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with our own trainers and staff who need it to serve you, or where we are required to do so by law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe and restrict access to authorised staff. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies & Third-Party Services

This website may load third-party content such as Google Maps and Google Fonts. These services may collect basic technical information such as your IP address in accordance with their own privacy policies.

7. Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Write to us at dg_fitnessclub@gmail.com or call +91 97654 82266 and we will act on your request within a reasonable time.

8. Children

Membership for individuals under 18 requires consent from a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect information from minors without such consent.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.
